# Logo glyphs

Abstract, brand-style marks offered as optional logos for custom providers (picked in the provider settings dialog).
They are original geometric constructions drawn for this extension (rings and knots, spirals and arcs, polygons and
lattices, node graphs, waves and signals, orbits, light and energy) and are released with the extension under its
MIT license. They deliberately avoid shapes that resemble the official vendor logos shipped in `../providers`.
